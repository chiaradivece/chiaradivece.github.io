I build real-time perception for things that move: first an ultrasound probe over a fetal brain, now soft robots. I lead AI at morph, a soft robotics startup still in stealth.

My PhD at UCL taught deep learning models to guide sonographers to the right views in fetal brain ultrasound, without any tracking hardware, in real time on NVIDIA's Clara AGX. Before that I built surgical simulators with haptics and VR in Milan and Chicago. I also trained in ballet for fifteen years.

Research

My PhD taught machines to find their way through fetal ultrasound. Now I build AI for soft robots.

The thread across all three: real-time perception, in imaging, in surgery and in simulation.

Navigating fetal ultrasound

Sensorless 6D plane pose regression that tells a sonographer how far they are from a diagnostic standard plane, using only the 2D video stream, deployed in real time (3 ms) on NVIDIA Clara AGX. Plus a multicentre benchmark for fetal biometry.

IEEE T-MRB {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %} IJCARS {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %} Scientific Reports {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %}

Simulation for surgical training

A position-based dynamics model of brain deformation for keyhole neurosurgery, and a VR and haptics trainer for Veress needle insertion.

IEEE RA-L {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %} IJCARS {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %}

3D vision inside surgery

Keypoint matching that sees beyond RGB with hyperspectral imaging, a robotic rig for multi-view laparoscopic capture, and a hard look at where image matching benchmarks break down.

HyKey {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %} Robotic arm platform {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %} Mismatched {% include icon.html name="arrow-up-right" %}
